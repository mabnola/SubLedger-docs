Your data belongs to you. SubLedger is built on a privacy-first foundation — your subscription information is never sold to third parties.

## SubLedger's privacy commitment

SubLedger collects only the information needed to operate the service: your subscription details, billing amounts, and account preferences. That data is used to power your dashboard and deliver renewal reminders — nothing more.

**SubLedger never sells your data.** Your subscription habits, spending patterns, and personal information are not shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for commercial purposes.

<Note>
  For the complete details of how SubLedger collects, uses, and protects your data, read the [SubLedger privacy policy](/support/privacy-policy).
</Note>

## Getting a copy of your subscription data

On the **Business plan**, you can export your subscription records yourself from the Reports page in CSV, XLSX, or PDF format. The export includes subscription names, categories, costs, offsets, effective costs, dates, and status.

<Info>
  Self-serve export is currently a Business plan feature. If you're on the Free, Personal, or Family plan and need a copy of your data, email [help@mysubledger.com](mailto:help@mysubledger.com) and the support team will help you.
</Info>

## Deleting your data

### Deleting individual subscriptions

You can permanently delete any subscription record yourself:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscription">
    From your dashboard, open the subscription's action menu and choose **Edit Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the record">
    On the edit page, click **Delete** and confirm when prompted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a subscription is permanent and cannot be undone. If you only want to stop tracking a subscription as active — while keeping its history — use **Cancel Sub** instead, which marks it cancelled but keeps the record.
</Warning>

### Deleting your whole account

Account deletion isn't currently a self-serve action in the app. To request that your account and all associated data be permanently deleted, email [help@mysubledger.com](mailto:help@mysubledger.com) from your account email address.

<Tip>
  If you're on the Business plan, export your reports before requesting deletion so you keep a personal record of what you were tracking.
</Tip>
