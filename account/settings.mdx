title: Update your SubLedger profile and notifications
description: Change your SubLedger email address and password, upload a profile photo, and configure renewal reminder preferences — all from the Profile page.
---

Your **Profile** page is where you manage your account: your email address, password, photo, plan, forwarding address, categories, and reminder preferences all live there.

## Opening your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to SubLedger">
    Go to [app.mysubledger.com](https://app.mysubledger.com) and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Profile">
    Click **Profile** in the top navigation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's on the Profile page

The page is organized into cards:

| Card | What you can do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Account** | See your display name, email, and current plan badge |
| **Plan & Billing** | See your current plan, open the billing portal, or view all plans |
| **Profile Photo** | Upload an image or paste an image URL |
| **Email Address** | Change the email address on your account |
| **Password** | Set a new password (email sign-ins only) |
| **Bookkeeper Access** | Invite or revoke a bookkeeper (Business plan) |
| **Email Forwarding** | Copy your unique receipt-forwarding address |
| **Renewal Reminders** | Turn reminder emails on and set your lead time |
| **Categories** | Create and delete custom categories (Business plan) |

## Account details

The **Account** card shows your display name, email address, and plan.

<Note>
  Your display name is read-only in SubLedger. If you signed in with Google, it comes from your Google profile — change it there and it updates here.
</Note>

## Changing your email address

From the **Email Address** card, enter your new address and click **Update email**. SubLedger sends a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect once you click it.

<Note>
  If you signed in with Google, your email is managed by Google and the card displays it read-only.
</Note>

## Password and security

The **Password** card lets you set a new password — enter your current password, then your new one twice. Passwords must be at least 8 characters.

If you've forgotten your password, click **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in screen. SubLedger sends a reset link to your account email.

<Note>
  If you signed in with Google, you don't have a SubLedger password and the Password card isn't shown. Your sign-in is managed through your Google account.
</Note>

## Renewal reminder preferences

The **Renewal Reminders** card controls whether SubLedger emails you before a subscription renews.

- Toggle **Email reminders** on or off.
- When on, use the **Remind me** dropdown to pick your lead time: 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days before renewal.

<Warning>
  Reminder emails are off by default — you have to switch them on once. See [renewal reminders](/subscriptions/reminders) for full details.
</Warning>

## Saving your changes

Most cards have their own action button (**Update email**, **Update password**, and so on). The Renewal Reminders toggle and dropdown save immediately as you change them.
