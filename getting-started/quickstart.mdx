---
title: "Quickstart: track your first subscription today"
description: Follow these steps to create a free SubLedger account, add your first subscription, and switch on renewal reminders in under five minutes.
---

SubLedger is designed to get you up and running quickly. By the end of this guide, you'll have a free account, at least one subscription on your dashboard, and renewal reminders switched on so you're notified before you're charged.

<Info>
  No credit card is required to create a free account. The free tier supports up to 10 subscriptions with renewal reminders and dashboard summaries included.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your free account">
    Go to [app.mysubledger.com](https://app.mysubledger.com). On the sign-in screen, click **Sign up**, then enter your email address and choose a password — or click **Continue with Google**.

    <Tip>
      Use the email address you want to receive renewal reminder notifications on — you can always update it later on your Profile page.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your first subscription">
    From the dashboard, click **New Entry** (or **Add Entry**). Fill in the details:

    - **Name** — the service you're being charged for (e.g., "Netflix", "Figma")
    - **Cost** — the amount you're charged each billing cycle
    - **Billing cycle** — weekly, every 4 weeks, monthly, quarterly, or yearly
    - **Renewal date** — when your next charge is due

    Click **Save** to add the subscription to your dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch on renewal reminders">
    Renewal reminders are **off by default** — turn them on once and they cover every subscription you track.

    Click **Profile** in the top navigation, scroll to the **Renewal Reminders** card, and toggle **Email reminders** on. Then use the **Remind me** dropdown to choose your lead time: 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days before renewal. Your choice saves immediately.

    <Note>
      The lead time is account-wide, not per subscription. See [renewal reminders](/subscriptions/reminders) for details.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review your spend summary">
    Your dashboard displays a **Monthly Spend** total and a **Yearly Estimate** at the top, plus a breakdown of your spend by billing cycle.

    <Note>
      SubLedger converts every subscription to a monthly equivalent — dividing yearly costs by 12, quarterly by 3, and so on — then multiplies the monthly total by 12 for the yearly estimate. See [spend summary](/dashboard/spend-summary) for the full calculation.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>
