---
title: Upgrade or downgrade your SubLedger plan anytime
description: Learn how to switch between SubLedger's Free, Personal, Family, and Business plans, and understand when each change takes effect.
---

You can change your SubLedger plan at any time from the **Pricing** page. Upgrades take effect immediately, giving you instant access to new features. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep full access through the time you've already paid for.

<Note>
  Plan changes are made on the **Pricing** page, reachable from the top navigation or from the **Plan & Billing** card on your Profile page. Payment details themselves are managed in the Stripe billing portal via **Manage billing**.
</Note>

## Upgrade your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pricing page">
    Sign in to [app.mysubledger.com](https://app.mysubledger.com) and click **Pricing** in the top navigation. Your current plan is marked, and all plans are listed with their features and pricing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose monthly or yearly">
    Use the **Monthly** / **Yearly** toggle above the plan cards to see prices for each billing interval.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select your new plan">
    Click the upgrade button on the plan you want.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete payment">
    If this is your first paid plan, you'll be taken to Stripe Checkout to enter your payment details. If you already have a paid plan, the change is applied to your existing subscription right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When you upgrade mid-cycle, SubLedger prorates the change — you're credited for the unused portion of your current plan and charged for the new one. Your billing date resets to the day of the upgrade.
</Note>

---

## Downgrade your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Pricing page">
    Sign in at [app.mysubledger.com](https://app.mysubledger.com) and click **Pricing** in the top navigation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select a lower plan">
    Click the downgrade button on the plan you want to move to.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Your current plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. After that, your account switches to the new plan automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A scheduled downgrade appears on both the Pricing page and the **Plan & Billing** card on your Profile, showing the date it takes effect. Changed your mind? Click **Cancel scheduled downgrade** any time before that date and you'll stay on your current plan.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Only one pending plan change can be scheduled at a time. Scheduling a new change replaces the previous one.
</Note>

## Switching to the free plan

Downgrading to Free follows the same process as any other downgrade, and takes effect at the end of your paid period. Keep in mind that the free tier supports up to 10 subscriptions, and features like employer reimbursement tracking, shared costs, subscriber names, bookkeeper access, custom categories, and reports are no longer available after the switch.

<Warning>
  If you're on the Family or Business plan and have assigned **subscriber names** to subscriptions, check with support before downgrading if you need to keep them — subscriber names are a Family-and-above feature and won't be editable on a lower plan.
</Warning>

## Switching between monthly and yearly billing

You can switch billing intervals from the same **Pricing** page without changing your plan tier — use the **Monthly** / **Yearly** toggle, then click the button on your current plan.

Because SubLedger treats any move to a higher-priced option as an upgrade and any move to a lower-priced one as a downgrade:

- **Monthly to yearly:** applies immediately, with proration. Your billing date resets to the day you switch.
- **Yearly to monthly:** applies at the end of your current annual period.

<Tip>
  Yearly billing gives you two months free — you pay for ten months and get twelve. Flip the **Yearly** toggle on the Pricing page to see each plan's effective monthly rate before you commit.
</Tip>

## What happens if you exceed your plan's subscription limit

If you downgrade to a plan with a lower subscription limit, the subscriptions over that limit become **locked**. You can still see them, but you can't edit them, and you can't add new subscriptions until you're back under the limit.

SubLedger keeps your oldest subscriptions active and locks the most recently added ones that exceed the limit. Locked entries show a **Locked — over plan limit** badge, and a banner appears on your dashboard with options to upgrade or manage your list.

<Note>
  Over-limit subscriptions are never deleted automatically. You can regain full access by upgrading your plan or deleting subscriptions until you're within the limit.
</Note>
