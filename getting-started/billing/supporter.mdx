description: If you're on the free plan and want to support SubLedger's development, you can make a one-time or recurring contribution at any amount you choose.
---

SubLedger's free tier is permanently free — no trials, no feature cliffs, no pressure to upgrade. If you find SubLedger useful and want to support its continued development, you can make a voluntary contribution from the pricing page.

## How it works

The contribution option appears at the bottom of the **Free plan** card on the [pricing page](https://app.mysubledger.com/pricing), under **Support our mission**. You choose:

- **Monthly** — an ongoing monthly contribution that you can cancel at any time.
- **One-time** — a single contribution with no recurring charges.

Set any whole-dollar amount you'd like — the minimum is $1 and the amount starts at $10. SubLedger processes contributions securely through Stripe, the same payment provider used for all paid plans.

## How to contribute

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the pricing page">
    Sign in at [app.mysubledger.com](https://app.mysubledger.com) and click **Pricing** in the top navigation, or visit the pricing page directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Support our mission">
    Look at the bottom of the Free plan card, below the **Get started free** button, for the **Support our mission** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your frequency and amount">
    Select **Monthly** or **One-time**, then set the dollar amount using the **−** and **+** buttons or by typing it in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    Click **Pay what you can** to proceed to Stripe checkout. Enter your payment details and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Contributing does not change your plan or unlock additional features. It's a way to support SubLedger while continuing to use the free tier.
</Note>

## Managing your contribution

If you set up a monthly contribution and want to cancel or adjust it, you can manage it through the Stripe customer portal. Contact [help@mysubledger.com](mailto:help@mysubledger.com) if you need help.
