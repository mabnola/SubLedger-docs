SubLedger offers four plans — Free, Personal, Family, and Business — so you can start at no cost and scale up as your needs change. All paid plans are available on monthly or yearly billing, and you can upgrade or downgrade at any time.

## Pricing at a glance

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly | Subscriptions | Standout feature |
|------|---------|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Up to 10 | Renewal reminders, dashboard |
| Personal | $10/mo | $100/year ($8/mo) | Up to 20 | Employer reimbursement tracking |
| Family | $20/mo | $200/year ($17/mo) | Up to 50 | Shared cost splitting |
| Business | $50/mo | $500/year ($42/mo) | Unlimited | Bookkeeper access, downloadable reports |

<Tip>
  Choosing yearly billing gives you **two months free** on any paid plan — you pay for ten months and get twelve.
</Tip>

<Note>
  On the Free plan you can also **pay what you can** — a voluntary monthly or one-time contribution at any amount. It doesn't change your plan or unlock features. See [supporting SubLedger](/getting-started/billing/supporter).
</Note>

<Note>
  SubLedger never sells your data or uses your subscription information for ad targeting, regardless of which plan you're on.
</Note>
