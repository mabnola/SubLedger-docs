title: "Spend summary: monthly and yearly cost estimates"
description: "See how SubLedger converts every billing cycle to a monthly equivalent, how offsets change your totals, and what's excluded from your spend summary."
---

The spend summary gives you an honest picture of what your subscriptions actually cost. SubLedger converts every active subscription to a monthly equivalent and adds them up, showing a **Monthly Spend** total and a **Yearly Estimate** at the top of your dashboard.

## How SubLedger calculates your totals

Every subscription is converted to a monthly equivalent based on its billing cycle:

| Billing cycle | Monthly equivalent |
|---------------|--------------------|
| Weekly | cost × 4.33 |
| Every 4 weeks | cost × 13 ÷ 12 |
| Monthly | cost |
| Quarterly | cost ÷ 3 |
| Yearly | cost ÷ 12 |

Your **Monthly Spend** is the sum of those monthly equivalents. Your **Yearly Estimate** is that monthly total multiplied by 12.

<Note>
  The yearly estimate assumes your current subscriptions remain unchanged. If you cancel or add a subscription mid-year, your actual annual spend will differ from the estimate.
</Note>

## Spending breakdown by billing cycle

Below your totals, the dashboard shows a **spending breakdown** grouping your subscriptions by billing cycle — weekly, every 4 weeks, monthly, quarterly, and yearly — so you can see how much of your spend is locked into each cadence. Cycles you aren't using are hidden.

<Tip>
  To break your spend down by category instead, use the category filter on the [Reports page](/dashboard/reports) (Business plan).
</Tip>

## How totals update

Your spend totals update whenever you:

- **Add a subscription** — the new amount is included in both totals right away.
- **Edit a subscription** — changing the cost, billing cycle, or offsets recalculates your totals.
- **Delete a subscription** — the cost is removed from your totals.
- **Cancel a subscription** — cancelled subscriptions stop counting toward your totals.

## Shared costs and employer reimbursements

If you record shared contributions or an employer reimbursement on a subscription, SubLedger subtracts those amounts before converting the cost to a monthly equivalent. Your totals are built from **effective costs** — what you're personally responsible for, not the full sticker price.

If offsets add up to more than the subscription's cost, the effective cost is treated as $0 rather than a negative number.

<Tip>
  See [shared costs and reimbursements](/subscriptions/shared-costs) for how to record offsets.
</Tip>

## What the spend summary does not include

- **Cancelled subscriptions** — once a subscription has a cancellation date, it's excluded from your totals and counted separately as cancelled.
- **Deleted subscriptions** — removed entirely, along with their history.
- **One-time purchases** — SubLedger tracks recurring charges only.

<Note>
  There's no separate "paused" state in SubLedger. To stop a subscription counting toward your totals while keeping its record, use **Cancel Sub** from its action menu — you can reactivate it later.
</Note>
