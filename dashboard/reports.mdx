Downloadable reports let you export a structured summary of your subscriptions — useful for expense reviews, tax preparation, or sharing with a finance professional. Reports are available on the **Business plan**.

<Info>
  If you're on the Free, Personal, or Family plan, you'll need to upgrade to Business to access reports. See [compare plans](/getting-started/billing/compare-plans) for a full feature breakdown.
</Info>

## What reports include

Each report covers the subscriptions in your account at the time of export. Reports contain:

- Subscription name and category
- Full cost and billing cycle
- Cost offsets (shared costs and reimbursements) and your effective cost
- Start date, renewal date, and status (active or cancelled)
- A summary row with totals for subscription count, full cost, offsets, and your cost

## Filtering your report

Before downloading, you can narrow the data using the filters at the top of the Reports page:

- **Period** — choose a preset (**Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **Last 12 months**, **All time**) or click **Custom** to pick your own date range.
- **Category** — limit the report to a single category, or leave it on **All categories**.
- **Status** — show **All**, **Active only**, or **Cancelled only**.

The summary row above the table updates as you apply filters, so you can see totals for the current view before exporting.

## How to download a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Reports">
    Sign in to SubLedger at [app.mysubledger.com](https://app.mysubledger.com) and click **Reports** in the top navigation. The Reports link appears only on the Business plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Use the period, category, and status filters to narrow your report to the data you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a format and download">
    Click the export button for your format: **CSV**, **XLSX** (Excel), or **PDF**. The file downloads to your device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  CSV is best for importing into other tools. XLSX preserves formatting for spreadsheet review. PDF generates a formatted table suitable for sharing with a bookkeeper or attaching to an expense report.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Subscriptions older than three years are archived automatically and drop out of new reports. Export anything you need to keep for long-term records.
</Note>

## Bookkeeper access

The Business plan also includes **bookkeeper access**, which lets you invite a bookkeeper or accountant to work with your subscription data directly — no need to forward files manually.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click **Profile** in the top navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Bookkeeper Access card">
    Scroll to the **Bookkeeper Access** card on your profile page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your bookkeeper">
    Enter your bookkeeper's email address and click **Send invite**. They'll receive an email with a link to accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Bookkeeper access is not read-only. Once your bookkeeper accepts, they can view **and edit** your subscriptions, offsets, and reports. They cannot change your plan or billing details, or invite other bookkeepers. See [bookkeeper access](/account/bookkeeper-access) for the full breakdown.
</Warning>
