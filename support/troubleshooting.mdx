Most issues in SubLedger have a straightforward fix. Work through the relevant section below — each one covers what causes the problem and exactly what to do about it. If none of these resolve your issue, [contact support](/support/contact) and include as much detail as you can.

## Login & account access

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password and can't log in" icon="lock">
    You can reset your password from the SubLedger sign-in screen.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Go to the sign-in page">
        Navigate to [app.mysubledger.com](https://app.mysubledger.com) and click **Forgot your password?** below the sign-in form.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter your account email">
        Type the email address you used when you created your SubLedger account and submit the form.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your inbox">
        Open the password reset email. If it doesn't appear within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set a new password">
        Click the link in the email, then enter a new password twice to confirm. Passwords must be at least 8 characters.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      If you signed up with Google, you don't have a SubLedger password — sign in with **Continue with Google** instead.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't log in even though my password is correct" icon="circle-alert">
    If you're certain your password is right but login is failing, try these steps in order:

    1. **Clear your browser cache and cookies**, then try logging in again.
    2. **Try a different browser or an incognito/private window** to rule out a browser extension conflict.
    3. **Check that caps lock is off** — passwords are case-sensitive.
    4. **Confirm you're using the right email address** — if you have multiple email accounts, you may be entering credentials for the wrong one.
    5. **Check whether you originally signed up with Google.** If you did, there's no password on the account — use **Continue with Google**.

    If none of these work, [email support](mailto:help@mysubledger.com) with your account email and a description of what you see when you try to log in.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reminder emails

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My renewal reminder emails are not arriving" icon="bell-off">
    The most common cause is that reminders were never switched on — they're off by default on new accounts.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Confirm reminders are switched on">
        Click **Profile** in the top navigation and scroll to the **Renewal Reminders** card. Make sure the **Email reminders** toggle is on. If it was off, no reminders have been sent.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check your spam folder">
        Search for emails from **noreply@reminders.mysubledger.com** in your spam, junk, or promotions folder. If you find them there, mark them as "not spam" so future reminders land in your inbox.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check the renewal date and lead time">
        Reminders go out based on the **Remind me** lead time in the same card — 7 days before renewal by default. If a renewal is closer than your lead time, the reminder for this cycle has already passed.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify your account email">
        On the **Profile** page, check that the email shown in the **Account** card is one you actually read.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Add **noreply@reminders.mysubledger.com** to your contacts or email whitelist so SubLedger reminders always reach you.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want to change how far in advance I receive reminders" icon="bell">
    Go to **Profile** and find the **Renewal Reminders** card. With **Email reminders** switched on, use the **Remind me** dropdown to choose 1, 3, 7, 14, or 30 days before renewal. The change saves immediately and applies to your next reminder.

    <Note>
      The lead time is account-wide — it applies to every subscription you track. SubLedger doesn't support a different reminder setting for individual subscriptions.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Subscriptions not saving or updating

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A subscription I added disappeared or won't save" icon="database">
    If a subscription entry vanishes after you save it or after a page refresh, try the following:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Check your internet connection">
        SubLedger requires an active connection to save changes. Verify you're online and try saving again.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reload the page and check again">
        Occasionally a display glitch can make an entry appear missing. Reload the page — if the subscription was saved successfully, it should reappear.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check whether you're at your plan limit">
        If you're over your plan's subscription limit, new entries are blocked. See the locked-entries section below.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Re-enter the subscription">
        If the entry is genuinely missing, add it again. Make sure you click **Save** before navigating away.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Do not navigate away from a subscription form before saving. Unsaved changes are discarded when you leave the page.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A subscription is locked and I can't edit it" icon="lock">
    If you see **Edit (locked)** on a subscription's action menu, your account is over its plan's active-subscription limit — usually after a downgrade.

    SubLedger keeps your oldest subscriptions active and locks the most recently added ones that exceed the limit. Locked entries are never deleted automatically.

    To unlock them, either upgrade your plan or delete enough entries to get back under the limit. See [upgrading and downgrading](/getting-started/billing/upgrade-downgrade).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What's the difference between cancelling and deleting an entry" icon="trash-2">
    SubLedger gives you two different actions, and they do different things. Neither one cancels the subscription with the provider — for that, log in to the provider's website directly.

    **Cancel Sub** marks the subscription as cancelled. The record stays in your list with its cancellation date, and it stops counting toward your spend totals. You can **Reactivate** it later from the same menu.

    To cancel an entry:

    1. Open the subscription's action menu (the three-dot button on its card).
    2. Select **Cancel Sub**.

    **Delete** removes the record permanently, along with its history.

    To delete an entry:

    1. Open the subscription's action menu and choose **Edit Details**.
    2. On the edit page, click **Delete**.
    3. Confirm when prompted.

    <Warning>
      Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone. If you might want the history later, cancel instead of deleting.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I need to change the billing cycle on a subscription" icon="refresh-cw">
    To update a billing cycle (for example, from monthly to yearly):

    1. Open the subscription's action menu and choose **Edit Details**.
    2. Change the billing cycle field to the new frequency.
    3. Update the **Renewal date** to match the new cycle if needed.
    4. Click **Save**.

    SubLedger recalculates your spend summary automatically to reflect the updated cycle.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I was charged but don't recognize the charge" icon="credit-card">
    Charges from SubLedger will appear on your statement as **MABLLC** or **Michele A Barard LLC**. If you see a charge you don't recognize:

    1. Check whether you recently upgraded from the Free plan.
    2. Log in to SubLedger, go to **Profile** → **Plan & Billing**, and click **Manage billing** to review your plan and payment history.
    3. If you still don't recognize the charge, [email support](mailto:help@mysubledger.com) with the charge amount and date so we can investigate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I update my payment method or billing information" icon="wallet">
    Payment details are handled by Stripe, SubLedger's payment processor.

    Go to **Profile** → **Plan & Billing** and click **Manage billing**. This opens the Stripe billing portal, where you can update your card, billing address, and view invoices. Changes take effect immediately.

    <Note>
      **Manage billing** appears only if you're on a paid plan.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want to downgrade or cancel my SubLedger plan" icon="arrow-down-circle">
    Go to the **Pricing** page in the top navigation and click the downgrade button on the plan you want. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep full access through what you've already paid for.

    A scheduled downgrade is shown on both the Pricing page and the **Plan & Billing** card on your profile, with a **Cancel scheduled downgrade** link if you change your mind before it takes effect.

    Downgrading to Free stops future charges and your data is retained — though entries over the new plan's limit become locked. See [upgrading and downgrading](/getting-started/billing/upgrade-downgrade).

    If you have trouble changing your plan, [contact support](mailto:help@mysubledger.com) and include your account email.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
