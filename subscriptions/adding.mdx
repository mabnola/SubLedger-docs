title: "How to add a new subscription in SubLedger"
description: "Learn how to add any recurring charge to SubLedger by entering its name, cost, billing cycle, and next renewal date — no credit card required to start."
---

Adding a subscription to SubLedger takes about 30 seconds. You enter a few pieces of information and SubLedger immediately folds the charge into your monthly and yearly spend estimates, and keeps the entry ready to categorize or share.

<Tip>
  You don't need every detail upfront. Start with the name and cost, then add the renewal date later when you have it in front of you.
</Tip>

## What you'll need

Before you start, have the following ready:

- **Name** — the service or product name (for example, "Netflix" or "Adobe Creative Cloud")
- **Cost** — the amount you're charged per billing period
- **Billing cycle** — how often the subscription renews (weekly, every 4 weeks, monthly, quarterly, or yearly)
- **Renewal date** — the date of the next upcoming charge

## How to add a subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Sign in at [app.mysubledger.com](https://app.mysubledger.com) and go to your dashboard. You'll see your current subscription list and a summary of your monthly spend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Entry">
    Click **New Entry** in the top navigation — or **Add Entry** from your dashboard or subscriptions list. The new subscription form opens on its own page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the subscription name">
    Type the name of the service in the **Name** field. Use the name you'll recognize when scanning your list later — for example, "Spotify" or "AWS".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the cost">
    Type the amount you're charged in the **Cost** field. Enter the amount for one billing period — if you pay \$99 per year, enter `99`, not a monthly equivalent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the billing cycle">
    Choose from **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Yearly**, or **Every 4 weeks** in the billing cycle selector. SubLedger uses this to calculate your monthly and yearly spend summaries correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the renewal date">
    Enter the date of your next charge in the **Renewal date** field. SubLedger uses this date to keep the entry current after each billing cycle, and to send renewal reminders if you've switched them on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the subscription">
    Click **Save**. The subscription appears in your list immediately, and your spend estimates update automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Optional fields

The subscription form also includes several optional fields you can fill in at any time:

- **Category** — label the subscription so you can filter reports by type. See [categories](/subscriptions/categories).
- **Start date** — the date you originally started this subscription. Useful for tracking how long you've been paying.
- **Website URL** — a link to the service's website or account page for quick reference.
- **Notes** — any additional details you want to remember about this subscription.
- **Cancellation date** — setting a cancellation date marks the subscription as cancelled in your list.
- **Subscriber name** — assign the subscription to a specific person in your household. Available on the [Family plan and above](/getting-started/billing/compare-plans).
- **Shared with others** — split the cost with contributors so your totals reflect your share. Family plan and above; see [shared costs](/subscriptions/shared-costs).
- **Employer reimbursement** — record an amount your employer covers. Personal plan and above; see [reimbursement](/subscriptions/reimbursement).

<Tip>
  You can add or edit optional fields later by opening the subscription's action menu in your list and choosing **Edit Details**.
</Tip>

## What happens next

Once saved, SubLedger:

- Adds the subscription to your dashboard and spend summary
- Rolls the renewal date forward automatically after each billing cycle
- Includes it in your renewal reminder emails, if you've [turned reminders on](/subscriptions/reminders)

<Note>
  Renewal reminder emails are off by default and are an account-wide setting, not a per-subscription one. Switch them on once in your profile and they cover every subscription you track.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Don't want to enter details manually? You can also [forward a receipt email](/subscriptions/email-import) and SubLedger will create the subscription entry for you automatically.
</Tip>
