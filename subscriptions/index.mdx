SubLedger gives you one place to track every recurring charge. This section covers how to add subscriptions, keep them organized with categories, handle costs you share or get reimbursed for, and configure renewal reminders so you're never caught off guard.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Adding a subscription" icon="plus-circle" href="/subscriptions/adding">
    Enter a subscription's name, cost, billing cycle, and renewal date in about 30 seconds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Categories" icon="tag" href="/subscriptions/categories">
    Assign categories like Entertainment, Utilities, or Software & Tools to label what each charge is for.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shared costs" icon="users" href="/subscriptions/shared-costs">
    Track plans you split with family members or subscriptions your employer reimburses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Renewal reminders" icon="bell" href="/subscriptions/reminders">
    Switch on email reminders and choose your lead time — reminders are off until you enable them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
