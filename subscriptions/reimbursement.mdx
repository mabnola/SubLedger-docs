---
title: Track employer-reimbursed subscriptions in SubLedger
description: "Record the amount your employer covers on a subscription so your SubLedger spend totals reflect what you're actually paying out of pocket. Requires the Personal plan or above."
---

If your employer covers the cost of certain subscriptions — like a software tool, a professional service, or a communication app — you can record the reimbursed amount in SubLedger. This keeps your spend summary accurate by counting only what you're personally paying.

<Info>
  Employer reimbursement tracking is available on the **Personal plan** and all higher plans. If you're on the Free plan, you'll need to upgrade to use this feature. See [compare plans](/getting-started/billing/compare-plans).
</Info>

## Why this matters

Your dashboard spend totals are most useful when they represent your actual out-of-pocket costs. Without recording reimbursements, your monthly and yearly estimates include charges you'll be paid back for — making your numbers less meaningful for personal budgeting.

## How to record a reimbursement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscription for editing">
    From your dashboard, find the subscription your employer reimburses, open its action menu, and choose **Edit Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand Employer reimbursement">
    On the form, click the **Employer reimbursement** section to expand it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the employer and amount">
    Enter your employer's name and the dollar amount they cover.

    <Tip>
      Reimbursements are recorded as dollar amounts, not as an on/off flag. If your employer covers the entire subscription, enter its full cost — that brings your effective cost to $0. If they cover part of it, enter just that part.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. The subscription stays in your list, and your spend totals drop by the reimbursed amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each subscription can have one employer reimbursement. You can record reimbursements on as many subscriptions as you like.
</Note>

## What changes in your dashboard

After recording a reimbursement:

- The subscription stays visible in your dashboard list, with the reimbursed amount shown on the **Employer reimbursement** section of its edit form.
- Your **monthly spend total** and **yearly estimate** drop by the reimbursed amount — not necessarily by the subscription's full cost.
- On the Business plan, reports show the full cost, the offset, and your effective cost as separate columns.

<Warning>
  SubLedger does not communicate with your employer or submit reimbursement claims on your behalf. This feature is for personal tracking only — it helps your spend numbers reflect reality.
</Warning>
