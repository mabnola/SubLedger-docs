---
title: Organize subscriptions with categories in SubLedger
description: Assign categories like Entertainment, Utilities, or Software & Tools to your subscriptions so you can filter reports and see at a glance what each charge is for.
---

Categories let you label your subscriptions by type. Each subscription's category appears on its card in your list, and on the Business plan you can filter and group your reports by category — useful for expense reviews and tax prep.

## Available categories

SubLedger includes the following built-in categories, available on every plan:

- **Entertainment** — streaming video, music, games, and other media services
- **Utilities** — internet, phone, electricity, and similar essential services
- **Software & Tools** — productivity tools, developer tools, and SaaS apps
- **Health & Fitness** — gym memberships, wellness apps, and fitness platforms
- **Finance** — budgeting tools, investment platforms, and financial services
- **Education** — courses, learning platforms, and training subscriptions
- **Food & Drink** — meal kits, delivery memberships, and coffee subscriptions
- **Other** — anything that doesn't fit the above

## How to assign a category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscription for editing">
    From your dashboard, open the subscription's action menu and choose **Edit Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a category">
    Click the **Category** dropdown and choose one from the list. Leave it set to **No category** if none fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The category appears as a badge on the subscription's card in your list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to change a category

Follow the same steps — open the subscription for editing, pick a different option from the **Category** dropdown, and save. There's no limit to how many times you can change a category.

## Where categories show up

- **On your subscription cards** — the category appears as a small badge next to the renewal date.
- **In reports** (Business plan) — filter a report to a single category, and see each subscription's category as a column in the CSV, XLSX, and PDF exports.

<Note>
  Your dashboard's spending breakdown groups subscriptions by **billing cycle** (weekly, every 4 weeks, monthly, quarterly, yearly) rather than by category. To break spend down by category, use the category filter on the [Reports page](/dashboard/reports).
</Note>

<Tip>
  Subscriptions left as **No category** show up with a blank category in reports. Assigning categories to everything makes your category-filtered reports complete.
</Tip>

## Custom categories

On the **Business plan**, you can create your own categories to match how you organize your spending. Custom categories appear alongside the built-in ones in the **Category** dropdown when you add or edit a subscription.

### Create a custom category

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click **Profile** in the top navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Categories">
    Find the **Categories** card on your profile page. Built-in categories are listed first, followed by any custom ones you've created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a category">
    Type a name in the **New category name…** field and click **Add**. It's available immediately when editing any subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Delete a custom category

Click the trash icon next to any custom category you've created.

<Note>
  Deleting a custom category doesn't delete the subscriptions using it — those subscriptions become uncategorized, and you can assign them a new category at any time. Built-in categories can't be deleted.
</Note>

<Info>
  Custom categories require the [Business plan](/getting-started/billing/compare-plans). Built-in categories are available on all plans.
</Info>
