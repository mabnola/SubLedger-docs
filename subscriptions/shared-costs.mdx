---
title: Track shared costs and reimbursements in SubLedger
description: Record the portion of a family plan you actually pay, or the amount your employer covers, so your spend totals reflect what comes out of your pocket.
---

Not every subscription you track is one you pay for entirely yourself. SubLedger lets you record two kinds of cost offsets: amounts covered by people you share a plan with, and amounts covered by your employer. Both keep your spend summary accurate — showing only what you actually pay.

<Info>
  Both work the same way underneath: you record a name and a dollar amount, and SubLedger subtracts it from the subscription's cost. Your spend totals use the remainder — your **effective cost**.
</Info>

## Splitting a plan with others

If you share a subscription and split the cost, you can record each person's contribution. This feature is available on the **Family plan and above**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscription for editing">
    From your dashboard, open the subscription's action menu and choose **Edit Details** — for example, a family streaming plan or a shared cloud storage account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand Shared with others">
    On the form, click the **Shared with others** section to expand it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contributors">
    Click **Add contributor**, then enter the person's name and the dollar amount they cover. Repeat for each person sharing the cost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. SubLedger subtracts the contributions from the subscription's cost and uses the remainder in your spend summary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Contributions are entered as **fixed dollar amounts**, not percentages. For a $15 plan split three ways, enter two contributors at $5.00 each — SubLedger then counts $5.00 as your share.
</Note>

<Warning>
  You can add up to **6 contributors** per subscription. Once you reach the limit, the **Add contributor** button is disabled.
</Warning>

## Tracking subscriptions for employer reimbursement

If your employer reimburses you for a work-related subscription, record the amount they cover so it's excluded from your out-of-pocket totals. This feature is available on the **Personal plan and above**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the subscription for editing">
    From your dashboard, open the subscription's action menu and choose **Edit Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand Employer reimbursement">
    On the form, click the **Employer reimbursement** section to expand it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the employer and amount">
    Enter your employer's name and the dollar amount they cover. If they cover the full cost, enter the full amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The reimbursed amount is subtracted from the subscription's cost in your spend summary.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each subscription can have **one** employer reimbursement, and it can be combined with shared contributors on the same subscription. Reimbursed subscriptions stay fully visible in your list — only the amount counted toward your spend changes.
</Note>

<Warning>
  SubLedger tracks what you tell it. It doesn't connect to your employer's reimbursement system or verify approval status. Keep your own records for reimbursement claims.
</Warning>

---

## How offsets affect your totals

For each subscription, SubLedger calculates:

```
effective cost = subscription cost − shared contributions − employer reimbursement
```

The result is never negative — if offsets exceed the cost, the effective cost is $0. Your monthly and yearly spend estimates are built from effective costs, so they always reflect what you personally pay.

<Tip>
  On the Business plan, reports show full cost, total offsets, and effective cost as separate columns — useful when you need to show both the sticker price and your share.
</Tip>
